# Unilever Industrial Engineering Competition

**Manufacturing improvement concepts · Engineering competition project · 2024–2025**

## Challenge
Develop engineering concepts to reduce changeover and material-handling time in a manufacturing operation.

## Proposed solutions

**SMED-based changeover concept**
- Applied workflow resequencing and quick-change component design.
- Proposed reducing changeover time from **120 to 16 minutes**.

**Material-handling concept**
- Developed a **proposed SAP-integrated slider and modular conveyor**.
- Targeted unloading in **under 10 minutes**, compared with a **45-minute** baseline.
- Estimated potential annual savings of **over EUR 150,000**, subject to the project's assumptions.

## Why this is relevant to MES / MOM
Operational data is useful only when connected to the actual production process. This concept links manufacturing workflow design with process visibility, integration ideas and measurable KPIs.

## Evidence and limitations
**These are proposed targets and estimated outcomes**, not implemented or independently verified factory results. No third-party confidential design files are shared here.
