# Fapofa Kft. Organizational Diagnosis and Process Improvement

**Academic team:** Hana H. Sayed, Shahd A. Negm, and Youssif Attia.

A wood-products company case study with PEST and SWOT analysis, organization mapping, process flowcharting, Ishikawa root-cause analysis and Pareto prioritization. Proposed actions use Lean methods, 5S, standardized work, inspection checklists and training.

**Skills:** quality improvement, organizational analysis, process management, Lean, root-cause analysis.

This academic case study does not establish audited company results. The team slide deck remains private pending review.
