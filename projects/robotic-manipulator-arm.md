# Four-DOF Robotic Manipulator Arm

Academic mechatronics project focused on a 4-DOF robotic arm for light industrial drilling. Includes CAD-based mechanical design, motor and encoder selection, a KiCad motor-controller PCB concept, and MATLAB/Simulink control modeling including PI, PID and cascade strategies.

**Skills:** CAD, mechatronic integration, PCB design, motor control, MATLAB and Simulink.

Source reports and drawings remain in the private archive because they include student-identifying records.
