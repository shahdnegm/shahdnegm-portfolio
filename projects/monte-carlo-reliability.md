# Monte Carlo Reliability Simulation

**Team:** Hana Hesham Sayed, Shahd Ayman Negm, and Youssif Attia.

Academic discrete-event Monte Carlo analysis of a three-machine production line with two intermediate buffers. The team examined stochastic failure and repair times, processing time, scrap, and maintenance policies. Candidate configurations covered buffer capacities of 10, 20, 30, 40, and 50 units per buffer, and reactive or preventive maintenance at 100, 150, 200 or 250 hours.

**Skills:** Python, Excel, reliability modeling, statistical distribution fitting, operations analysis.
